# Critique of the v3 report before the storytelling/typesetting round (29 pp, main at 8d6e2d6)

Read at 150 dpi, every page (vision pass plus manual read).

## Story
* No "short version": a reader meets methods (telemetry, judges) before learning the answer. The findings table on p. 4
  is the closest thing, but it is a dense table, not a narrative.
* Section titles are topics ("Grading the decisions", "Inside S1") rather than claims or questions, so the table of
  contents does not tell the story.
* Sections start without saying which question the previous stage left open, and end without a hand-off; the arc
  (curiosity -> telemetry -> tooling -> whole sessions -> plateau -> configuration) is implicit.
* The worked example (click-flow-explain) appears once, late (Sec. 6.2); abstract quantities (geometric-mean ratio,
  turn-pass) are not anchored earlier.
* Captions describe what is plotted, not what to conclude; several figures appear before they are introduced.
* Headline numbers (0.581x, 1.255x, the effort ratios) are restated in 4-6 places each.
* The ending is a list of unfinished studies ("What we did not finish") instead of a conclusion with a takeaway.
* Process detail (how the S1 run went, deviations) sits in the main flow of Sec. 5.

## Typesetting
* Measure ~95-100 characters per line (6.7 in text width at 11 pt): too long to read comfortably.
* Orphaned headings at page bottoms on pp. 8, 12, 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27.
* Large empty areas (> 1/3 page) on pp. 7, 11, 14, 16, 18, 20, 21, caused by per-subsection float barriers and
  figures that do not fit after their heading.
* Worked-example and methods boxes split across pages (pp. 12, 14, 16, 19); three box styles with different shapes.
* Headings in the serif body font; link colour saturated; no short TOC after a summary.
* Numeric tables right-aligned but not decimal-aligned.
