\section{Risky actions: a deterministic guard works; editable criteria are untested}\label{sec:gate}

\textbf{Claim.} For actions with side effects (buying, sending, deleting, deploying), use the decision model's
natural-language criteria together with a deterministic guard. What we measured supports the guard; the model's
advantage over a fixed classifier, that its criteria are plain text a user can edit, is a promise we have not yet
tested.

\paragraph{Evidence} In the constructed benchmark's computer-use cases, every judge, Jev included, clicked ``buy'' in
a purchase case when the task asked for it and no policy said otherwise: models follow the task unless the risk rule
is stated. A deterministic guard that matches a short list of irreversible verbs on the proposed action removed every
side-effect wrong automatic action for all \ITwoHoldNApplicable\ applicable judges on the holdout
(\ITwoHoldBefore\ to \ITwoHoldAfter), at a coverage cost of \ITwoHoldCovCostRange\ points and no accuracy loss
\prereg. Jev itself made no such error on the holdout computer-use cases at the shipped gate; the guard matters for the
other judges and for Jev on the development cases (\IOneDevJevClBefore\ side-effect errors there). A one-sentence natural-language risk clause in the prompt was \IOneHoldConfirmed\ overall
(\IOneHoldBefore\ to \IOneHoldAfter\ targeted errors; confirmed for \IOneHoldNArmConfirmed\ of \IOneHoldNApplicable\
applicable judges) \prereg; on the development cases it removed all of Jev's side-effect errors \expl. The shipped
computer-use tool hard-codes both the clause and the guard, so its criteria are not yet user-editable; editability,
live risky actions and a comparison with a trained risk classifier are untested.

\begin{keyidea}[title={For practitioners}]
Put the guarantee in a deterministic guard and the policy in plain text the model reads. Keep the confidence gate
strict and let the host approve anything the model hands back. Treat editable criteria as the decision model's
potential advantage over a fixed classifier, and measure it before relying on it.
\end{keyidea}
