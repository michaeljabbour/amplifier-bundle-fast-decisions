\section{What real-usage telemetry tells you, and what it cannot}\label{sec:telemetry}

\textbf{Claim.} Production telemetry is the right instrument for latency, usage mix and how often routing actually
applies, and the wrong one for accuracy or savings. In real use routing applied far less often than in the lab.

\paragraph{Evidence} One owner's harness recorded \ObEvents\ events over \ObDays\ days, \ObProdSessions\ of them
production sessions (\cref{tab:data}) \expl. A Jev decision
took \ObJevPfifty\,ms at the median against \ObHostPfifty\,s for a host call, \ObJevHostXFifty\X\ faster
(\ObJevHostXNinetyfive\X\ at p95; \cref{fig:latency}). In production the workspace-size gate kept \ObStScopePct\,\% of
sessions on the host, and Jev routed \ObJevRouteProd\,\% of the sessions it judged, against \AoFableJevRoute\,\% in
the main campaign and \ObJevRouteTest\,\% of test sessions. Effort was switched \ObEffSwitchProd\ times in
\ObEffReqProd\ production requests (against \ObEffSwitchTest\ in \ObEffReqTest\ test requests), and no production
session ran on Fable, the host where routing pays. The bundle's record of how often its proposals matched the host's
next action was a usage signal, not a correctness label: \ObProdRate\,\% in production against \ObTestRate\,\% in test
traffic, because most host actions could not have matched by construction (the headline \ObAllRate\,\% agreement was
mostly such unmatchable decisions).

\paragraph{A corrected projection} Before S1 we projected that the recommended configuration would cost
\AthreeOld\X\ (\AthreeOldBand) of shipped defaults on this owner's spend mix. That projection assumed medium effort
saves about as much on Opus as on Sonnet, which S1 did not demonstrate (\SHFourRatio\X). Recomputed on the same mix
(\AthreeOpusShare\,\% Opus, \AthreeSonnetShare\,\% Sonnet spend), with Opus at default effort and Sonnet hosts at
medium (\EcRatio\X), the projection is \AthreeNew\X\ (\AthreeNewBand) \expl; the correction is documented in the
supplement.

\begin{keyidea}[title={For practitioners}]
Instrument your harness for latency, the host-model mix and how often each gate fires. Do not read accuracy or
savings from it: measure those with labelled cases and paired sessions. Expect real usage to route less often than a
benchmark suggests.
\end{keyidea}
